# Histoire 80 — « La table basse » (écriture automatique, ton de la tornade)

Cinq auteurs en parallèle (police, armée, justice, État, escalade totale), trois juges (comédie, rétention, fabrication), réécriture de la gagnante.

## Classement du jury

- « La table basse » (justice) : 8, 8.6, 8.5 ; moyenne 8.4 ; 3 voix
- « Le yaourt » (police) : 7.5, 7.8, 7.5 ; moyenne 7.6 ; 0 voix
- « La fourmi » (armee) : 6.5, 8, 6 ; moyenne 6.8 ; 0 voix
- Le cerf-volant (total) : 6.5, 7, 7 ; moyenne 6.8 ; 0 voix
- La crêpe (etat) : 5.5, 6, 6 ; moyenne 5.8 ; 0 voix

## Texte retenu

Prémisse : Un homme se cogne l'orteil contre sa table basse et y voit un acte prémédité : les forces spéciales et l'armée arrêtent la table, la justice la condamne, mais en appel on lui trouve un bleu et c'est lui qui finit dans sa cellule.

 1. Ce mec s'est cogné l'orteil contre sa table basse.  
    *Image : Low angle at floor level: the man's bare foot slams into the leg of the low wooden table and he springs up, hopping on one foot and clutching his toe. The table sits in the foreground and does not move.*
 2. C'était prémédité.  
    *Image : Slow push-in on the table alone in the empty living room as a ceiling lamp swings over it and yellow evidence markers pop up around its four legs.*
 3. Les forces spéciales ont encerclé l'immeuble.  
    *Image : Night: masked special forces slide down ropes from a helicopter and form a ring around a small apartment block while the searchlight pins one window.*
 4. La table a refusé d'obtempérer.  
    *Image : Inside the living room a flashbang goes off in a white flash. The smoke clears and the table is exactly where it was, lit like a suspect.*
 5. Alors un char est entré par la façade.  
    *Image : Street level: an army tank drives straight into the building's front wall, which bursts into a cloud of bricks.*
 6. Il est ressorti trois immeubles plus loin.  
    *Image : Wide shot of three buildings in a row with the same round hole punched through all three. The tank bursts out of the side of the third one with the table balanced on its barrel.*
 7. Quatre paires de menottes.  
    *Image : Four soldiers, each handcuffed to one table leg, carry the table off like a stretcher. Behind them the tank driver, half out of his hatch, hops on one foot holding his toe.*
 8. Le pilote du char a été décoré.  
    *Image : A military band plays as a general pins a medal on the same tank driver. He salutes while balancing on one leg, his other foot in a big bandage.*
 9. Au procès, elle n'a exprimé aucun remords.  
    *Image : Slow push-in on the table in the dock between two gendarmes. One gendarme shifts and glances at it; the table stays perfectly still.*
10. La défense a plaidé qu'elle était là avant.  
    *Image : A lawyer in a robe unrolls a giant floor plan of the living room down the courtroom aisle. The table is drawn in its spot with a trail of footprints walking straight into it, and the whole jury leans in at once.*
11. Vingt ans ferme.  
    *Image : The judge slams his gavel and the CONDAMNÉE card comes down on the table like a giant stamp, squashing its legs flat for one frame before they spring back.*
12. En prison, elle a récidivé.  
    *Image : Top-down shot of the cell: three prison guards hop in a circle around the motionless table, each one clutching a foot.*
13. Trois gardiens. Perpétuité.  
    *Image : The three guards stand in a row on crutches, feet in casts, and salute as a giant stamp slams onto the cell door.*
14. En appel, la table a passé une radio.  
    *Image : Hospital room: the table lies on its back with its legs in the air on an X-ray bed, and the screen behind it lights up with a glowing X-ray of a table.*
15. Elle avait un bleu.  
    *Image : Extreme close-up of one table leg: a round blue bruise on the wood, and a yellow evidence marker pops up beside it.*
16. Ce mec a été condamné pour coups et blessures.  
    *Image : The man now stands in the dock. The gavel falls and the COUPABLE card slams across his chest, so hard that he staggers.*
17. Et pour les trois immeubles.  
    *Image : The courtroom's back wall falls flat behind the judge and shows the three buildings in a row, the same round hole punched through all three.*
18. Sa codétenue, c'est la table.  
    *Image : A guard shoves the man into a cell so narrow that the table stands between him and his bunk, and the barred door slams shut.*
19. Il s'est recogné le premier soir.  
    *Image : Night in the cell, framed exactly like shot one: same low angle, table in the foreground. His foot hits its leg and he springs up hopping on one foot, clutching his toe. The table does not move.*

Cartons : ARRÊTÉE (line 7), CONDAMNÉE (line 11), COUPABLE (line 16)

## Changements de la réécriture

- Merged original lines 5 and 6 into « Alors un char est entré par la façade. », as all three judges asked. « Alors » keeps the deadpan cause and effect: she refused, so a tank came in.
- Restaged « Il est ressorti trois immeubles plus loin. » as a single action: a wide shot with the same round hole through three buildings and the tank bursting out of the last one with the table on its barrel. It no longer packs three actions into one shot.
- Added the success beat the format requires, « Le pilote du char a été décoré. », modelled on candidate 1's medal line. « Pilote de char » is the correct French military term and is shorter than « conducteur ». The driver is the soldier who hops in shot 7 and then salutes on one foot in shot 8, so the gag and the medal share one character. This also sets up the hero paying for buildings that a decorated man destroyed.
- Cut « L'orteil a été placé sous scellés. ». It was the beat the panel valued least, and cutting it takes the script from 20 to 19 lines and about 185 syllables (around 32 to 34 s spoken). It also removes one of the four courtroom shots in a row.
- Kept « La défense a plaidé qu'elle était là avant. » because two of the three judges called it quotable and it sets up the reversal on appeal. It is restaged physically: the lawyer unrolls a floor plan with the hero's footprints walking into the table, and the jury leans in together, instead of a talking head.
- Replaced the arithmetic line « Trois gardiens, dix ans de plus. » with the fragment « Trois gardiens. Perpétuité. », so the appeal reverses a bigger sentence. Its image is guards in casts on crutches saluting as a stamp hits the cell door, replacing the static chalk tally marks.
- Replaced the plot line « En appel, un expert a examiné la table. » with « En appel, la table a passé une radio. », a table lying legs up on an X-ray bed in the hospital room. « Elle avait un bleu. » is kept word for word as the punch.
- Line 16 now says « Ce mec a été condamné… » instead of « Il a été… ». After a run of « elle » lines it makes clear in the audio that the hero is now the one convicted, and it echoes the hook.
- Line 17: dropped the held-up photo, which is unreadable on a phone. The courtroom's back wall now falls away to show the three holed buildings.
- Line 18 is rewritten as « Sa codétenue, c'est la table. ». It applies prison vocabulary to the object, puts « la table » in the punch position and is four syllables shorter. It is staged as an action: the man is shoved into a cell so narrow that the table stands between him and his bunk.
- Kept « Il s'est recogné le premier soir. » as the last line, as all three judges asked, with no « Ah, et » alternative. It is shot as an exact copy of shot one so the loop reads on mute and invites a rewatch.
- The table never moves in any shot. Each of its still shots gets a different camera and one small event so they do not repeat: evidence markers popping up, a flashbang, a gendarme shifting.
- The toe gag stays cartoon: hopping, bandages and casts only, with no blood and no swollen toe. The darkness stays at the reference's level: three buildings as collateral, no deaths.
- Nothing was grafted from the other candidates, because none of their lines fits this story's logic without bringing in their premise. Only the structure of the medal beat comes from candidate 1.
- To check with the showrunner: whether the series' « une bosse » episode is a story about walking into something at home. If it is, change line 15 to « Elle avait une éraflure. ». Also, if candidate 1 is produced, change its ending (for example to « Entre-temps, quelqu'un a mangé le yaourt. ») so two episodes do not both end with the hero sharing a cell with the object.

## Deuxième : « Le yaourt »

A man's yoghurt goes missing from the office fridge, so the police hold the fridge in custody, get a tip from the butter, break forty wrong doors and hand out medals, until the yoghurt turns up behind the butter and the man who complained gets two years in the fridge's cell.

- Ce mec s'est fait voler son yaourt au bureau.
- La police a envoyé trente voitures.
- Le frigo a été placé en garde à vue.
- Il n'a rien dit.
- Alors ils l'ont débranché.
- Le beurre a balancé un stagiaire.
- Le plaignant a désigné celui qu'il aimait le moins.
- Les forces spéciales ont défoncé sa porte.
- Ce n'était pas la bonne.
- Ni les quarante suivantes.
- Le stagiaire a tout avoué.
- Il a aussi avoué trois braquages.
- Le commissaire a été décoré.
- Le beurre aussi.
- Le lendemain, on a retrouvé le yaourt.
- Derrière le beurre.
- Le plaignant a pris deux ans.
- Dans la cellule du frigo.
- Ah, et le yaourt était périmé.

## Autres pistes

- « La fourmi » (armee) : A man finds a single ant in his kitchen and calls the army; after an air strike flattens two buildings and leaves the ant unharmed, the ant gets a peace treaty that gives it the kitchen and gives him the doormat.
- La crêpe (etat) : A man's first crêpe sticks to the ceiling and won't come down, so the State treats it as a national crisis: it wipes out the neighbourhood trying to remove it, decorates the crêpe for bravery and starts a war when the minister re-enacts the flip at a summit, and television calls every disaster good news.
- Le cerf-volant (total) : A man's kite gets stuck in a tree, and the police, the army and the courts treat the tree as a thief, flattening everything around it except the tree.
