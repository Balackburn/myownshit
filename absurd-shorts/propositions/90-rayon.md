# Histoire 90 — « Le rayon »

Écrite par trois auteurs en parallèle (science et sport, armée et État, médias et administration), départagée par trois juges (comédie, rétention, fabrication), corrigée selon leurs notes et vérifiée contre tous les épisodes publiés.

Prémisse : Ce mec a le soleil dans les yeux. L'agence spatiale réunit trois cents savants et déploie un parasol de la taille du pays dans l'espace, qui plonge tout le pays dans le gris. Le pays proteste à la lampe de poche, les gendarmes passent les menottes aux lampes, puis un char de l'armée perce un seul trou dans le parasol : au-dessus de son jardin, en plein dans ses yeux. Il avait un parasol au garage.

## Texte final

1. Ce mec avait le soleil dans les yeux.  — *The hero squints on his garden deckchair, hand over his eyes, a big bright sun right behind his head; low camera at lawn height.*
2. L'agence spatiale a réuni trois cents savants.  — *Space agency control room: rows of white-coated box-headed scientists at screens, every screen showing the same photo of the hero squinting; slow lateral pan.*
3. Solution retenue : un parasol dans l'espace.  — *Whiteboard diagram: sun, Earth and a huge beach parasol drawn between them; a scientist taps the parasol with a pointer; snap zoom.*
4. Douze fusées ont décollé de son jardin.  — *Twelve rockets lift off from the hero's lawn in a cloud of flame; the hero watches from his doorway holding his deckchair; wide low shot.*
5. Ses tomates tournent autour de la Terre.  — *Space: the hero's vegetable patch, stakes and red tomatoes, drifting in orbit past a satellite, Earth below; slow drift.*
6. Le parasol fait la taille du pays.  — *A giant red-and-white striped beach parasol snaps open in space in front of the sun; cut to its round shadow fitting exactly over a made-up, generic country shape on the globe (never a real outline).*
7. En s'ouvrant, il a fauché trois satellites.  — *The parasol's edge sweeps three satellites away, pieces tumbling into the dark; side tracking shot.*
8. Tout le pays est devenu gris.  — *Aerial view of the generic country: the round shadow slides across and pastel towns turn grey block by block; ÉTEINT card pops in.*
9. Les tournesols ont baissé la tête.  — *A field of sunflowers droop their heads all at once; low ground camera.*
10. Les panneaux solaires ont été mis au chômage.  — *Rows of solar panels on little legs queue in front of an employment office door, each holding a small cardboard sign; static wide shot.*
11. L'agence a déclaré la mission réussie.  — *Press room: the agency director at a podium, scientists applauding behind him, a screen showing the grey country; flashbulbs.*
12. Le pays a manifesté à la lampe de poche.  — *A grey city square packed with box-headed protesters all holding torches up to the sky, beams criss-crossing; low angle.*
13. Les gendarmes ont passé les menottes aux lampes.  — *Gendarmes walk down the line of protesters and snap handcuffs on each torch; the protesters hold out their torches calmly; tracking shot on the cuffs clicking.*
14. Un char a percé un trou dans le parasol.  — *An army tank in the grey country cranks its barrel straight up at the sky and fires; cut to space: the shell punches a neat round hole through the striped parasol; PERCÉ card pops in.*
15. Un seul.  — *One single beam of sunlight shoots down through the hole from space; vertical tilt down along the beam.*
16. Au-dessus de son jardin.  — *Top-down shot: the beam lands exactly on the hero's lawn, one bright disc in a completely grey country.*
17. En plein dans ses yeux.  — *Back on the deckchair: the hero squints, hand over his eyes, lit by the beam, exactly as in shot one; same framing as line 1.*
18. Le pays entier pique-nique chez lui.  — *The hero's garden packed edge to edge with picnicking crowds, blankets and baskets, towels spread over his car and roof; the hero squeezed in his deckchair in the middle; crane up.*
19. Il avait un parasol au garage.  — *The hero squeezes out of the packed garden into his garage, pulls a small dusty beach parasol off a shelf and opens it over his deckchair among the picnickers; static medium shot, deadpan.*

Cartes : ÉTEINT (line 8), PERCÉ (line 14).

Gag récurrent : Everything the State does lands on the hero's garden: the rockets lift off from it, his tomatoes go into orbit, and the only hole in the parasol falls on it, back in his eyes.
