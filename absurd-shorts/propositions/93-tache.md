# Histoire 93 — « La tache »

Écrite par trois auteurs en parallèle (science et sport, armée et État, médias et administration), départagée par trois juges (comédie, rétention, fabrication), corrigée selon leurs notes et vérifiée contre tous les épisodes publiés.

Prémisse : A man stains his shirt with tomato sauce; the stain refuses to leave, the State evicts it with riot police and a tank firing stain remover, the blast bleaches the whole neighbourhood and stretches the stain into the exact shape of the country, so the shirt becomes the national flag, and the man still has to iron it.

## Texte final

1. Ce mec a fait une tache sur sa chemise.  — *The hero in the white shirt (collar, buttons and breast pocket always clearly in frame) drops a forkful of pasta; a small irregular tomato orange-red splat lands on his chest, with a strand of pasta stuck to it and one drip. Push-in on the stain (never a round disc).*
2. La tache a refusé de partir.  — *At the sink, the hero scrubs the stain with a sponge; the stain does not move; he scrubs harder and the sponge falls apart in his hand.*
3. Le juge a signé son expulsion.  — *Courtroom: the shirt stands on a hanger in the dock with the stain facing the judge; the judge bangs the gavel and an eviction order is stamped.*
4. Les gendarmes mobiles ont chargé la chemise.  — *Low tracking shot: a line of riot police with round shields and visors charges across an empty courtyard at the shirt hanging alone on a clothes rack.*
5. La tache s'est retranchée sous la poche.  — *Extreme macro on the shirt: the splat slides up and under the breast-pocket flap; behind, out of focus, the riot police stop with shields raised.*
6. Un char a tiré au détachant.  — *An army tank with a giant stain-remover spray bottle strapped to its barrel fires a thick white jet at the shirt; recoil, dust.*
7. Il a décoloré tout le quartier.  — *Wide shot: the white jet sweeps past the shirt over the pastel neighbourhood, which turns pure white house by house.*
8. Les maisons, les voitures, le maire.  — *Three quick beats in one shot: white houses, white cars, then the mayor on the town hall steps, completely white down to his sash, blinking, unhurt.*
9. La tache, elle, est restée rouge.  — *Slow push-in: the shirt on its rack in a totally white street; only the stain is still red, but the jet has stretched it into a long irregular splash with drips and a pasta strand, all trailing in the jet's direction.*
10. Étirée, elle avait la forme exacte du pays.  — *Forensic officers in white suits run tape around the stretched splash; a transparent map of an invented country (made-up coastline, not any real country's outline) is laid over it and matches drip for drip; the officers step back.*
11. Un décret présidentiel interdit de la laver.  — *Press room: the president with his sash signs a decree at the podium and holds up a crossed-out washing machine sign; flashes pop.*
12. La chemise a remplacé le drapeau.  — *Low angle on the presidential palace roof: soldiers fold away a bundle of old cloth (never shown unfurled) while the white shirt (collar, buttons and breast pocket always clearly in frame) goes up the flagpole, the long country-shaped splash with drips across its chest; it reads as a shirt, not a flag.*
13. Deux gardes la saluent jour et nuit.  — *Split day/night in one shot: two ceremonial guards stand at the foot of the pole saluting the shirt; the sky snaps from day to night and they do not move.*
14. Les ambassades ont officiellement reconnu la tache.  — *A row of ambassadors in plain dark suits and sashes with no emblems or flags step up one after another and sign a register under the shirt on its pole; a military band plays.*
15. La chemise reste à ce mec. L'entretien aussi.  — *Two soldiers hand the hero an iron and an ironing board, with an official form on top; he holds them flatly.*
16. Il la repasse autour de la tache.  — *Static wide shot at dawn on the palace roof: the hero irons the shirt on a board, carefully steering the iron around the country-shaped splash; the two guards salute behind him.*

Cartes : EXPULSÉE (line 3), DÉCOLORÉ (line 7), HISSÉE (line 12).

Gag récurrent : Whatever the State fires, sprays or hoists, the stain stays red while everything around it changes (a white neighbourhood, a white mayor); the State's own blast is what gives it the country's shape, and the State ends up saluting the thing it shot at.
