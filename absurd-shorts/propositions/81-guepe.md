# Histoire 81 — « La guêpe »

Écrite par la boucle d'écriture (cinq auteurs, un par angle : police, armée, justice, secours et État, escalade totale ; jury de trois : comédie, rétention, fabrication), puis départagée et peaufinée.

## Premier tour

- « La mouche » (total) : 7, 8.6, 8 ; moyenne 7.9 ; 1 voix
- « Le détecteur » (secours) : 7.5, 6.8, 8.5 ; moyenne 7.6 ; 1 voix
- « L'oignon » (justice) : 8.5, 7.1, 7 ; moyenne 7.5 ; 1 voix
- « La guêpe » (armee) : 8, 8, 6.5 ; moyenne 7.5 ; 0 voix
- « Le sandwich » (police) : 7.5, 7.8, 7 ; moyenne 7.4 ; 0 voix

« La mouche » gagnait, mais reprenait des tournures de « La table basse » (« a refusé d'obtempérer », « le pilote a été décoré », un char, perpétuité) et le retournement du « Bocal » (une voisine règle en un geste ce que l'État n'a pas pu). Une réécriture sans ces redites est tombée à 5,8 de moyenne : propre, mais sans escalade.

## Départage

- C : moyenne 6.8 ; 2 voix
- B : moyenne 6.7 ; 1 voix
- A : moyenne 4.8 ; 0 voix

A = « La mouche » du premier tour ; B = sa réécriture sans redites ; C = « La guêpe » (volet armée). Juges : comédie C (7,5), rétention C (7,5), fabrication B (8). Le gagnant a ensuite été réécrit avec les notes des trois juges, contrôlé par deux vérificateurs (redites et règles ; français et images), puis corrigé.

Dernière retouche (la mienne) : les vérificateurs avaient retiré l'avion de chasse parce qu'il rappelle l'épisode de la tornade ; c'est justement le modèle demandé. L'avion revient, et il décolle du porte-avions coincé dans l'évier (« Un avion de chasse a décollé de l'évier. »), puis « Il a raté la guêpe. / Il a rasé l'immeuble. ».

## Texte retenu

Prémisse : Ce mec porte plainte contre une guêpe dans sa cuisine ; l'État lui fait une guerre totale, la perd, lui cède le sucrier et se distribue des médailles, à elle aussi. Mais c'était une mouche.

L'insecte est le même modèle du début à la fin : une mouche gris foncé aux ailes claires, sans rayures ni dard, petite dans le cadre jusqu'au dernier plan (la narration seule dit « guêpe » ; la révélation ne triche pas).

 1. Ce mec a porté plainte contre une guêpe.  
    *Image : Kitchen at eye level with the sugar bowl centred on the breakfast table: the box-headed man jabs a finger at a small dark insect on its rim while a police officer beside him writes in a notepad. The insect is the same model in every shot: a dark grey fly with clear wings, no stripes and no stinger, kept small in frame until the last shot.*
 2. La police a chargé.  
    *Image : Riot police burst through the kitchen door behind shields and plates explode off the shelves. The man ducks with his cup while the insect hovers above the sugar bowl, untouched.*
 3. Il a exigé une frappe aérienne.  
    *Image : The man slaps a request form with a big red stamp and a plane icon onto the notepad of the officer from shot one, who now wears a dented riot helmet. Dented riot shields lie on the floor behind them.*
 4. Un satellite a repéré la guêpe.  
    *Image : A satellite in orbit swivels its lens down toward the Earth below, and a green glint lights up in the lens.*
 5. Près du sucrier.  
    *Image : Satellite view straight down through the roof: a red map pin drops onto the sugar bowl, beside a single dark dot on its rim.*
 6. Un porte-avions a pris la mer.  
    *Image : A huge plain grey aircraft carrier with an invented roundel and one gun turret on its bow cuts through the open sea, its horn blasting.*
 7. Dans l'évier.  
    *Image : The carrier is jammed in his kitchen sink, its bow over the counter and water flooding the tiles. The man watches with his cup in his hand.*
 8. Un avion de chasse a décollé de l'évier.  
    *Image : The jet is catapulted off the deck of the aircraft carrier jammed in the sink and swoops low across the kitchen, past the man and his cup.*
 9. Il a raté la guêpe.  
    *Image : Slow motion: the small dark insect hovers calmly above the sugar bowl as the jet's two missiles streak past on either side of it and punch into the kitchen's back wall, which cracks.*
10. Il a rasé l'immeuble.  
    *Image : Wide street shot of the aftermath: dust rolls off a heap of rubble where his building stood, with the fridge and the carrier in its sink sticking out of it. The man sits on top in his chair holding his cup, and the last piece of wall topples beside him.*
11. Et la mairie, par précaution.  
    *Image : Down the street the town hall folds flat and its flag flops onto the rubble, but the flagpole stays standing.*
12. La guêpe a fini son sucre.  
    *Image : On the smoking rubble the sugar bowl stands on a scrap of kitchen tiles with its lid off, and the small insect dips into the sugar and keeps eating.*
13. Le front s'est figé entre le frigo et l'évier.  
    *Image : Low shot along the bare kitchen tiles on top of the rubble heap, with no walls or roof, from behind the soldiers' sandbags by the carrier in the sink: helmets in the foreground, the man small in his chair alone in no man's land, and opposite him the sandbagged fridge with a tiny flag marking the insect's line. The one action is a helmet peeking over the sandbags.*
14. Tout l'hiver, le sucre a été rationné.  
    *Image : Snow falls into the trench by the carrier, and an officer walks down the line handing each shivering soldier a single sugar cube.*
15. L'État a demandé un cessez-le-feu.  
    *Image : A white handkerchief on a stick rises slowly out of a hatch on the carrier stuck in the sink.*
16. La guêpe a obtenu le sucrier.  
    *Image : On the kitchen tiles in the rubble, a blank treaty sheet with no readable text lies under the sugar bowl as a plain backdrop, and the insect pushes a tiny flag into the lid. SIGNÉ card.*
17. L'état-major s'est distribué des médailles.  
    *Image : A row of generals stands at attention in the snowy rubble, their chests so loaded with medals that the whole row slowly tips forward. No band plays, and no one is shown pinning a medal on anyone.*
18. Même à la guêpe.  
    *Image : Over a general's shoulder, his huge fingers pin a tiny medal on the insect on top of the sugar bowl, and it salutes with a front leg. DÉCORÉE card.*
19. C'était une mouche.  
    *Image : Macro, framed like shot one: the insect with its medal on the rim of the sugar bowl is plainly a fly, with big red eyes, rubbing its front legs. Behind it, out of focus, the snow-covered man sips from his cup.*

Cartons : SIGNÉ (ligne 16), DÉCORÉE (ligne 18)

Narration placée : 34,39 s (19 phrases).

## Changements du peaufinage

- Line 3 [echo]: accepted. Changed to « Il a exigé une frappe aérienne. ». Dropping « Alors » removes the reference's opener, and the phone call is gone. He now slaps a stamped request form with a plane icon onto the notepad of the officer from shot one, who is wearing a dented riot helmet. The wall phone prop is cut. The request is for an air strike and the state sends a carrier that ends up shelling his kitchen. That mismatch is deliberate: it is the institution's logic.
- Line 4 [echo + staging]: both accepted. Changed to « Un satellite a repéré la guêpe. ». The crosshair and the « cible » wording are gone from shots 4 and 5. Shot 4 is one orbit-scale action: the satellite swivels its lens down and a green glint lights up. Shot 5 is one roof-scale action: a red map pin drops onto the sugar bowl.
- Line 6 [echo]: accepted. Changed to « Un porte-avions a pris la mer. », which breaks the « X a envoyé un Y » pattern and sets up « Dans l'évier. ». The carrier now has a bow gun turret from its first appearance so that shot 8 reads.
- Line 8 [echo + staging]: took the echo fix over the staging flag's option to keep the line, because « avion de chasse » is the reference's exact wording and its central shot. Changed to « Le porte-avions a tiré sur la guêpe. » with one action: the carrier's bow turret fires two shells from the sink. « Il l'a ratée. » still agrees (il = porte-avions, la = guêpe), and so does « Il a rasé l'immeuble. ». The fighter jet and the jet roundel are cut.
- Lines 9 and 10 [staging]: accepted. In shot 9 the shells pass on either side of the insect and punch into the kitchen's back wall, so his building coming down follows. Shot 10 shows only the aftermath: dust rolling off the rubble, the man on top in his chair, and the last piece of wall toppling as the one action. The fridge and the carrier in the sink stick out of the heap to set up the front.
- Line 12 [echo]: accepted. « Le sucrier n'a rien. » becomes « La guêpe a fini son sucre. ». The insect calmly dipping into the sugar on the rubble moves the beat from a household object being invulnerable (bocal) to the enemy's indifference. INTACT is dropped and no card replaces it. Two cards are within the limit. « son sucre » and « le sucre a été rationné » two lines later echo each other on purpose: the insect eats freely while the army is rationed.
- Line 13 [staging, both flags]: accepted and merged. The bare kitchen floor now sits on top of the rubble heap, with no walls or roof, and the fridge and the carrier in the sink are the two positions. The shot is low from behind the soldiers' sandbags: helmets in the foreground, the man small in no man's land, and the sandbagged fridge with a tiny flag opposite. Barbed wire is cut. The one action is a helmet peeking over. That tiny flag sets up the one planted in shot 16. The line is unchanged.
- Line 14 [flat]: accepted. « Ça a duré tout l'hiver. » becomes « Tout l'hiver, le sucre a été rationné. », which turns the time-skip into the institution's logic about the thing the war is over. Image: an officer hands each shivering soldier one sugar cube in the snow. The hero is not in this shot, which avoids a fifth 'man sits with cup' frame. Winter still explains his snow-covered head in shot 19.
- Line 16 [french]: accepted. « Elle » becomes « La guêpe », so the antecedent is clear straight after « L'État ». The repeated name also feeds the reveal. « La guêpe » now occurs six times. That is deliberate: the narrator keeps insisting on the wrong species until « C'était une mouche. ».
- Line 16 [staging]: accepted. The footprint and the squiggle are dropped. The blank treaty sheet is a plain backdrop under the sugar bowl, and the one action is the insect pushing its flag into the lid. SIGNÉ stays on this line. The treaty is on the kitchen tiles in the rubble, consistent with shots 10 and 13.
- Line 17 [echo, both flags]: took the second fix. « Tout le monde a été décoré. » becomes « L'état-major s'est distribué des médailles. », and line 18 becomes « Même à la guêpe. ». This drops table's « a été décoré » construction and centime's band. The joke now targets a general staff that lost and decorated itself anyway. Image: a row of generals in the snowy rubble, chests so loaded with medals that the row tips forward, with no band and no pinning. The only pinning in the episode is still on the insect in shot 18, which keeps the DÉCORÉE card.
- Line 19 [echo]: accepted. « Ah, et c'était une mouche. » becomes « C'était une mouche. », so it no longer copies the reference's closing words. The macro frame that mirrors shot 1, and so loops the video, is kept word for word.
- Unchanged word for word: lines 1, 2, 5 (text), 7, 11 and 15 and their staging, and shots 18 and 19 apart from the line 18 wording.
- Result: 19 lines of 2 to 8 words, about 99 words, about 32-35 s spoken. Two one-word title cards (SIGNÉ on 16, DÉCORÉE on 18), no exclamation marks, no real organisation or insignia.

## Autres candidats

- « Le sandwich » (police) : A man whose sandwich disappears from the office fridge files a complaint for aggravated theft, and the police respond with custody for the fridge, a lineup, a special-forces raid on a whole building, twenty years for a toothless pensioner and a medal for the dog, until the sandwich turns up inside the complainant.
- « La guêpe » (armee) : A man finds a wasp in his kitchen and calls the army, which fights it as an enemy power: an aircraft carrier in his sink, an air strike that flattens his building and the town hall, a winter in the trenches and a ceasefire that gives the wasp the sugar bowl. Everyone gets a medal, he pays for the carrier, and it was a fly all along.
- « L'oignon » (justice) : A man cries while chopping an onion and files a complaint for assault, so the State arrests, tries and jails the onion, until on appeal it pleads self-defence and the man goes to prison for assault with a knife.
- « Le détecteur » (secours, second du premier tour) : A man calls the firemen because his smoke detector keeps chirping, and the State's full emergency plan floods his flat, flattens his building, decorates a tank and finally jails both the detector and him in the same cell.
