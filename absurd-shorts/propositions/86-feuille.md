# Histoire 86 — « La feuille »

Écrite par la boucle d'écriture : cinq nouveaux auteurs (police, armée, justice, secours, escalade totale), plus « Le fromage », troisième de l'histoire 85, remis en compétition. Le gabarit « guerre contre la nuisance » était interdit (trop utilisé) : rouages de l'État autres que militaires.

## Jury de l'atelier

- « Le distributeur » (total) : 7.5, 8, 7 ; moyenne 7.50 ; 0 voix

- Le fromage (retour) : 5.5, 5, 4 ; moyenne 4.83 ; 0 voix

« La feuille » a gagné, mais trois audits indépendants ont trouvé qu'elle refaisait « La table basse » (80) : blessure par un objet, intention criminelle, forces spéciales, objet arrêté, procès avec légitime défense, appel, prison, héros blessé à nouveau à la fin. Le second, « Le distributeur », refaisait « Le paquet de chips » écarté (prise d'otages, char à bout portant, rebond).

## Restructuration puis relance

Première correction : la feuille se cache parmi ses sœurs, un référendum sur la broyeuse que cinquante millions de bulletins rejettent. Elle avait perdu les dégâts collatéraux et la chute. Relance en trois versions (escalade, chute, images), jugées avec la version restructurée :

- escalade : 8.3, 8.4, 8.3 ; moyenne 8.33 ; 3 voix
- images : 7.6, 7.2, 7.2 ; moyenne 7.33 ; 0 voix
- chute : 6.5, 6, 6 ; moyenne 6.17 ; 0 voix
- actuelle : 5.5, 5, 4.5 ; moyenne 5.00 ; 0 voix

Audit final appliqué : plus de procès ni de verdict, le stagiaire clairement adulte, la feuille relâchée devient la lettre de licenciement du héros, qui vendait des broyeuses.

## Texte final

1. Ce mec s'est coupé avec une feuille.  — *Medium close-up at a shop sales counter: the hero is frozen over his raised index finger, which has a thin red line, and on the counter lies the sheet with a dog-eared corner and a tiny red smear. Slightly soft behind him are a demo shredder with a dangling price tag and a wall of identical boxed shredders, with no sign, word or brand.*
2. Elle s'est fondue dans la foule.  — *The sheet tilts her dog-eared corner toward the demo shredder, then slides herself into the open ream on the shelf, smear down, and vanishes among identical edges while his hurt finger hovers over the stack, lost.*
3. Signalement : blanche, rectangulaire.  — *Street: a policeman slaps RECHERCHÉE wanted posters onto a row of lampposts, each one showing nothing but a blank white rectangle, while the hero walks beside him holding up his plastered finger.*
4. La police a embarqué tout le papier du pays.  — *A police-van ramp: an endless single file of paper climbs in (reams, newspapers, books, a rolled map), each item holding a tiny numbered mugshot board, and the RECHERCHÉE posters, peeled off their lampposts, bring up the rear. Card EMBARQUÉ.*
5. Et tous les billets de banque.  — *A bank vault being emptied: stacks of plain banknotes march up the same van ramp holding tiny numbered mugshot boards, a police dog sniffs the last one, and the bank director stares at the bare shelves.*
6. Et le stagiaire, pour recel de malfaiteur.  — *The shop intern, clearly an adult (thirties, tie, lanyard, briefcase at his feet), still hugging the empty ream wrapper, is bent calmly over a police car bonnet and cuffed by a bored officer.*
7. Le doigt l'a reconnue.  — *Line-up room: five identical sheets stand against the height chart. Behind the glass his bandaged finger rises on its own and points at the third, and when an officer flips her the smear shows and the dog-ear springs up.*
8. Le procureur a requis la broyeuse.  — *Courthouse steps, press scrum: the sheet is held upright between two gendarmes at the top of the steps. The prosecutor, at a cluster of microphones, whips a velvet cloth off a shredder on a trolley, the same model as the shop's demo with the price tag still dangling, and she curls back, trembling. No courtroom, no dock.*
9. Le président a soumis la broyeuse à référendum.  — *Press room: the president in his sash at the podium taps a pointer on a poster of the same price-tagged shredder, which has a OUI box and a NON box.*
10. Cinquante millions de bulletins.  — *A school gym buried waist-deep in white ballot papers identical to her, with officials wading through and pushing snow shovels.*
11. Une forêt entière.  — *A hillside of fresh stumps to the horizon: a gendarme with a chainsaw salutes beside the last log as it is loaded onto a truck marked BULLETINS.*
12. Les bulletins ont tous voté non.  — *Polling station during voting: a queue of voters hold their ballots over the transparent OUI box, and every ballot wriggles out of their fingers and dives into the transparent NON box, while the OUI box stays empty.*
13. Le sien en premier.  — *Close-up insert at the same polling station, wall clock at eight, both transparent boxes still empty: the hero holds his ballot over the OUI box, bandaged finger well away from it, and the ballot twists out of his fingers and is the first to dive into the NON box.*
14. La broyeuse a été abolie.  — *Courthouse steps: two ushers throw the velvet cloth back over the trolley shredder and wheel it away down the steps, and between the two gendarmes the sheet stops trembling. Card ABOLIE.*
15. Les gendarmes ont aplati toutes les broyeuses.  — *A vast empty square laid out with rows of identical price-tagged shredders: a gendarme drives a ribboned steamroller over them and flattens them like biscuits, with a puff of confetti behind each, while a line of gendarmes salutes. There is no building and no shop front.*
16. La feuille a été relâchée.  — *Courthouse steps: one gendarme opens his fingers and the sheet drifts away down the steps on a draught, past the press microphones. No judge, no gavel, no handcuffs.*
17. Reconvertie en lettre de licenciement.  — *The shop's back office, beside a bare paper shelf: the manager (adult, tie) has the sheet in a typewriter and the keys hammer LICENCIEMENT across her, next to the dog-ear and the tiny red smear. It is a typewriter, never a printer or photocopier.*
18. Adressée à ce mec.  — *The sales counter of shots 1 and 19, from the manager's side: the manager slides the sheet, folded in three, across the counter toward the hero, who takes it at arm's length by the far edge, bandaged finger lifted away. No postman, no envelope, no front door.*
19. Il vendait des broyeuses.  — *Exact framing of shot one, the sales counter: the spot where the demo shredder stood is bare except for its price tag lying on it, and the wall behind is empty. He holds the unfolded letter toward camera so LICENCIEMENT and the dog-ear read first (the red smear tiny), with his bandaged finger raised away from its edge and no new cut. Hold about a third of a second longer than the other shots.*

Cartes : EMBARQUÉ (line 4), ABOLIE (line 14).

Gag récurrent : The price-tagged demo shredder from the hero's shop keeps coming back as the State's weapon: on the prosecutor's trolley, on the referendum poster and in the rows under the steamroller. In the last shot it is gone from his own counter, and only its price tag is left. After the line-up, the bandaged finger he points with stays carefully away from every piece of paper (his ballot, the dismissal letter). The hero ends up ruined but is never cut again.
