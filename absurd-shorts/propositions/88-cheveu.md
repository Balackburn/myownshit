# Histoire 88 — « Le cheveu »

Prémisse : Un cheveu trouvé dans une soupe est arrêté, fiché et interrogé ; pour lui trouver un propriétaire, la police rase tout le quartier, puis le ministre s'excuse et le cheveu retourne dans la soupe.

## Texte final

1. Ce mec a trouvé un cheveu dans sa soupe.  — *Restaurant table: the box-headed hero holds his spoon over a bowl of soup; a single black hair floats on top. Low close-up on the bowl, the hero's face behind it.*
2. Un hélicoptère de police a éclairé la soupe.  — *Night exterior through the restaurant window: a police helicopter hovers and its searchlight cone lands exactly on the soup bowl. Low angle up from the table.*
3. Le cheveu a été arrêté à la pince.  — *A policeman in gloves lifts the hair out of the soup with giant tweezers, held at arm's length; a second officer holds an open evidence bag. Tight two-shot.*
4. Photographié de face, puis de profil.  — *Mugshot wall with height lines: the hair stands upright holding a tiny number board; flash, quarter-turn, flash again, it looks exactly the same. Locked frontal camera.*
5. Interrogé douze heures sous la lampe.  — *Interrogation room: the hair alone on a metal chair, a big lamp bent down at it, a detective leaning on the table; a wall clock spins fast. Slow push-in.*
6. Il a frisé. Ça a été retenu contre lui.  — *Under the hot lamp the straight hair coils into a tight curl; the detective writes in a file and slams a stamp on it. Close on the hair, then on the stamp.*
7. Les chiens ont remonté sa piste jusqu'au cuisinier.  — *Two police sniffer dogs on leashes drag their handlers out of the restaurant kitchen door and up a street to a front door. Low tracking shot behind the dogs.*
8. Ils ont enfoncé sa porte.  — *Masked officers swing a battering ram; the cook's front door flies flat into the hallway. Wide, camera at floor level.*
9. Et toutes celles de la rue.  — *Long street of houses: every front door falls inward one after the other like dominoes as the ram team runs down the pavement. Wide lateral dolly.*
10. Le cuisinier était chauve.  — *The cook in his white jacket, toque in hand, head perfectly bald and shining under a torch beam held by an officer. Static medium shot.*
11. Il a été rasé quand même.  — *An officer runs electric clippers over the already bald head; nothing falls; the cook stands still, toque in hand. Tight shot on the head.*
12. Puis les clients et les passants, pour comparaison.  — *Outside the restaurant, a line of customers and passers-by sit on chairs as officers shave them with clippers; hair piles grow on the pavement, each labelled with a tag. Wide high angle.*
13. Le chien policier aussi.  — *One of the sniffer dogs sits fully shaved, pink and smooth, beside its handler, still at attention. Static, dog centred.*
14. Ce mec, deux fois.  — *The hero, already bald, sits in the chair; the clippers pass over his head, pause, and pass a second time. Frontal close-up.*
15. Au microscope : aucune correspondance.  — *Forensic lab: a scientist in a white suit at a microscope, the soup hair under one lens, shelves of labelled hair bags behind; a red cross pops on the screen. Push-in on the screen.*
16. Le ministre s'est excusé auprès du cheveu.  — *Press room: the minister at the podium bows deeply toward the hair, which stands on a small stand next to the microphones; cameras flash. Wide from the press seats.*
17. C'était le dernier cheveu du quartier.  — *Aerial over the neighbourhood: a crowd of shining bald heads and the bald dog, and in the middle the hair standing alone on its stand. Slow descending crane.*
18. L'hélicoptère l'a reposé.  — *Night: the police helicopter hovers over the restaurant and lowers the hair on a winch cable, searchlight on, through the open window. Low angle up.*
19. Dans la soupe de ce mec.  — *Back at the table: the bald hero sits before the same bowl; the hair lands on the soup and floats. Same framing as shot one, held.*

Cartes : FICHÉ (line 4), FRISÉ (line 6), CHAUVE (line 10).

Gag récurrent : The State applies full criminal procedure to a single hair (searchlight, tweezers arrest, mugshot, interrogation, forensic match) and shaves everyone who could own it, bald people included, while the hair itself is treated as a citizen owed an apology; the helicopter that lit the soup at the start puts the hair back in it at the end.
