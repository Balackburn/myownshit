# Histoire 85 — « La place »

Écrite par la boucle d'écriture : cinq nouveaux auteurs (police, armée, justice, secours, escalade totale), plus « Le coq », deuxième de l'histoire 84, remis en compétition. Interdits pour cet épisode, parce que « Le barbecue » et « La rambarde » les ont utilisés : pompiers et grande échelle, blague du rez-de-chaussée, tir à la corde, catastrophe naturelle, président qui donne un ordre à un objet, évacuation par hélicoptère, démineurs, funérailles nationales, monument historique, héros arrêté à la fin, objet saisi ou loué par l'État, alerte nucléaire, masques à gaz, parachutistes, lance-flammes, quarantaine, espion, fête nationale de la nuisance, dernière phrase en « Ah, et… ».

« Le paquet de chips » a gagné, mais l'éditeur l'a écarté : c'est la même histoire que « Le bocal » (78) avec un autre objet (impossible à ouvrir, char, rebond, ouverture facile par un tiers, dernière phrase qui dégonfle). Le second, « La place », est passé en réécriture.

## Réécriture de « La place »

Trois réécritures (clarté, gags physiques, noirceur sèche), chacune vérifiée contre les épisodes publiés, puis jury de trois :

- clarte : 8.3, 8.1, 7.6 ; moyenne 8.00 ; 3 voix
- physique : 7.4, 7.6, 7.3 ; moyenne 7.43 ; 0 voix
- sec : 7.8, 7.1, 6.6 ; moyenne 7.17 ; 0 voix

Un audit indépendant a trouvé que le premier jet refaisait « La guêpe » temps pour temps (l'ennemi qui finit son goûter, le traité qui donne l'objet à un autre, la récompense de l'ennemi en fragment final). Corrigé : protection des témoins, sommet de quarante tours sur le rond-point, zone neutre, blocus jamais levé ; carte PLIÉ au lieu de RASÉ ; acheteurs évacués visiblement.

## Texte final

1. Ce mec s'est fait piquer sa place au supermarché.  — *Supermarket car park, low camera behind the hero's bumper: a small red car zips into the last free bay one second before the hero's car, which brakes hard with its indicator still blinking.*
2. L'armée a confirmé l'invasion.  — *War room built as a single map-table prop: a general stabs a red flag-pin into a tiny red toy car parked in a painted bay on the car-park map while the hero stands stiffly beside him, pointing at it.*
3. La marine a imposé un blocus au rond-point.  — *A grey warship sits on the grassy central island of the roundabout; red-and-white striped booms slam down across every exit and its cannon swings to check each car as it goes round.*
4. Ultimatum : dix minutes pour se rendre.  — *Wide shot of the supermarket front: a giant red countdown clock on the roof flips to 10:00 and starts ticking while soldiers march the shoppers out, hands up, pushing their full trolleys across the road to the far pavement beyond the roundabout; tanks aim at the doors behind them.*
5. L'envahisseur était au rayon fromage.  — *Inside the emptied store, the red car's driver stands alone in the cheese aisle calmly sniffing a big plain wheel of cheese (smooth rind, no holes) while a red laser dot wanders across his back.*
6. Six avions ont bombardé sa voiture.  — *Six fighter jets in formation swoop low over the car park and drop their bombs straight onto the little red car.*
7. Et les deux cents places autour.  — *High aerial shot: every car in the car park is squashed flat as a pancake inside its painted bay, smoking in neat rows, a lone hubcap rolling across. At the edge, in the driving lane, the hero's car stands untouched with its indicator still blinking. Beyond the roundabout the shoppers stand safe in a row behind a barrier on the far pavement, holding their full trolleys and watching.*
8. Le supermarché aussi. Il était mal garé.  — *The empty supermarket folds flat like a cardboard box and its big sign drops on top, the countdown clock still attached; a puff of dust greys the invader, who already stands outside the doors with the cheese wheel under his arm. Card PLIÉ stamps.*
9. L'envahisseur a été placé sous protection des témoins.  — *Two masked special forces walk the grey, dusty invader away from the folded store with a grey blanket over his box head and the cheese wheel under his arm, and put him in the back of a black car, while the hero, beside his blinking car, points at him in outrage.*
10. La place a été reprise.  — *In the middle of the flattened rows the red car's bay is swept clean and empty; a soldier pushes a flagpole straight down into it (no famous diagonal flag-raising pose) and the hero, beside it, salutes proudly. Card REPRISE stamps.*
11. Trois mille soldats ont défilé dessus.  — *A military band and rows of saluting soldiers march in a tight loop inside the white lines of that single bay while the hero salutes at its edge. A plain military parade: no mayor, no ribbon, no tourists, no medals.*
12. Les autres pays ont convoqué un sommet.  — *A line of black official cars with little flags on their bonnets queues at one of the roundabout's striped booms while the warship's cannon swings from one car to the next to check it.*
13. Le sommet a duré quarante tours.  — *The black cars circle the warship nose to tail; leaders in sashes lean out of their windows passing the treaty pages from car to car, and a big lap counter on the ship's bridge clicks to 40.*
14. Le traité a déclaré la place zone neutre.  — *Back at the bay, the world leaders stretch striped tape around its white lines while the hero, still saluting at the edge, slowly lowers his hand. Card NEUTRE stamps.*
15. Chaque pays y a garé un char.  — *A tall tower of tanks, each flying a different little flag, is stacked inside the taped-off bay; the hero stands next to it beside his car, indicator still blinking.*
16. Ce mec paie les réparations de guerre.  — *The hero shakes his empty wallet over a general's upturned helmet; behind them a line of soldiers holding out their helmets stretches across the flattened car park to the horizon.*
17. Le blocus n'a jamais été levé.  — *Months later, grass has grown tall around the roundabout's striped booms and the warship is still on its island; the hero's car waits at the give-way line, indicator blinking, while the cannon swings round and stops on him.*
18. Il se gare dans le pays voisin.  — *A border post with a striped barrier: the hero ducks under it on foot toward camera, a shopping bag in each hand, while far behind him his car sits tiny in a lone painted bay under another country's flag, its indicator still blinking. Hold this shot a beat longer than the others.*

Cartes : PLIÉ (line 8), REPRISE (line 10), NEUTRE (line 14).

Gag récurrent : The hero's indicator, still blinking from the hook: his car is untouched in the flattened car park, it blinks next to him while the invader is led away, next to the tank tower, at the closed boom of the roundabout, and one tiny orange blink in the foreign bay in the last shot. Second gag: the invader's plain cheese wheel is unharmed through the cheese aisle, the folding store and his removal under witness protection.
