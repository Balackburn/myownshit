# Histoire 87 — « Le chat »

Atelier : « Le chat » 8,0 (devant « Le cheveu » 7,7 et « Le tonnerre » 7,3, produits en 88 et 89). Audit de structure appliqué (deux contrôleurs, correction, contre-audit).

Prémisse : A man's cat won't come down from a tree. The State treats it as a hostage-taking: commandos, a tank at point-blank range and three days of airstrikes. The only casualty is one twig. The man is arrested for feeding the enemy and his croquettes are seized as evidence. The cat is tried in absentia at the foot of the tree and ordered to come down. That night it does, on its own, to eat the seized croquettes. Then it climbs back up to sleep. The soldiers sent to escort it are still stuck in the branches.

## Texte final

1. Ce mec a un chat qui ne descend plus.  — *A cat on the top branch of a tall lone tree; below, the man points up and shakes a full bowl of croquettes*
2. Le chat retenait l'arbre en otage.  — *Police cars skid into a ring round the tree, an officer wraps police tape round the trunk, the man behind the tape with his bowl*
3. Aucune revendication.  — *A negotiator raises a megaphone to the treetop; the cat licks its paw; the megaphone slowly drops*
4. Les forces spéciales ont donné l'assaut.  — *A helicopter hovers; a masked commando slides down a rope and lands on the branch right beside the dozing cat, which does not even open its eyes*
5. Ils ne descendent plus non plus.  — *The tree covered in masked commandos clinging to the branches, the cat calm at the top; the helicopter reels in empty ropes and leaves; card COINCÉS*
6. Un blindé a visé le chat. À bout portant.  — *A tank's barrel rises and extends until its muzzle stops a hand's width from the cat's nose on the top branch; the cat blinks*
7. Le chat s'est baissé. Pas la rue d'en face.  — *The cat calmly lowers its head; the shell passes through the empty spot where its head just was, without touching it; behind the tree the opposite side of the street folds into a smoking ruin*
8. Trois jours de frappes aériennes.  — *Jets dive past the tree, explosions flash behind it, the sky flips day-night-day (no aerial crater-field shot)*
9. Bilan : l'otage a perdu une brindille.  — *Two medics in helmets carry a single twig on a stretcher under a foil blanket past a spokesman at a podium reading the toll; smoking rubble out of focus behind; the cat grooms at the top*
10. Ce mec a été arrêté pour avoir nourri l'ennemi.  — *He crawls through the rubble with his bowl; riot police pile on him; an officer holds the bowl up to flashbulbs like a seized haul; card SAISI*
11. Le chat a été inculpé pour les dégâts.  — *A bailiff staples a summons to the trunk; the cat's ear twitches*
12. Jugé au pied de l'arbre, par contumace.  — *A judge's bench in the rubble at the foot of the tree, the bowl on it with a red SCELLÉ tag; the bailiff reads to an empty dock while the cat's tail dangles above the judge's wig*
13. L'otage a demandé à rester avec le chat.  — *One image: a branch slowly curls round the dozing cat like a protective arm; in the foreground, only the clerk's microphone held up to the bark*
14. Condamné à descendre.  — *The judge bangs the gavel and points straight down at the ground; up on the branch the cat yawns; card CONDAMNÉ*
15. Escorté par les soldats.  — *From the foot of the tree the bailiff staple-guns an escort order onto each clinging commando's boot; one salutes and nearly slips*
16. Le soir, le chat est descendu manger les scellés.  — *Night: the cat walks head first down the trunk past the clinging commandos and their stapled escort orders, hops onto the empty judge's bench and crunches the croquettes in the SCELLÉ bowl*
17. Puis il est remonté se coucher.  — *Held dawn shot: the cat climbs back up past the commandos, still clinging, one still saluting, the escort orders flapping on their boots, and curls up inside the curled branch at the top; the empty sealed bowl on the bench below*

Cartes : COINCÉS (line 5), SAISI (line 10), CONDAMNÉ (line 14).

Gag récurrent : Nobody who goes up the tree comes down, except the cat. It comes down only once, when the court orders it to, and only to eat the evidence. Then it goes straight back up to bed. The bowl of croquettes runs through the episode: shaken in shot 1, seized from the man who 'fed the enemy' at L10 (card SAISI), sealed on the bench at L12, eaten at L16, empty in the last shot. The soldiers ordered to escort the cat are still clinging to the branches at dawn, one still saluting. The cat sleeps at the top in the branch that wanted to keep it.
